inspecting rendered pages or extracted text. No fixes are included in M7; nothing here is a human rating.

## Not problems (checked)

* No missing, duplicated or reordered paragraphs in PDF (Latin text) or DOCX (all scripts); no geometry

  headings are Word heading styles; DOCX QA ran (no UNVERIFIED) on every document.

* No score-only "improvements": verdicts use score-independent facts; none was flagged.
